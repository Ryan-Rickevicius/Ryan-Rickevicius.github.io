I'm Ryan Rickevicius, a student at Quinnipiac University pursuing dual degrees in Business Analytics and Sport Management. I've always been interested in understanding why things happen, whether that's what drives a team's performance, how data can reveal patterns, or how businesses can use information to make smarter decisions.

My passion for analytics has given me the opportunity to work with several sports organizations, including Quinnipiac University's Division I men's basketball and baseball programs, the Westfield Starfires, and the Wichita Falls NetBurners. Through these experiences, I've worked with performance data, developed tracking systems, and helped turn complex information into insights that coaches can actually use.

Beyond working with teams, I've also explored the research side of analytics. One of my most meaningful projects involved analyzing MLB Statcast data to better understand how pitch sequencing affects a hitter's ability to make contact. That experience strengthened my interest in statistical modeling, programming, and finding answers to questions that aren't always obvious.

While sports first sparked my interest in analytics, my goals extend beyond the sports industry. I'm especially interested in business analytics, machine learning, and how data can help organizations solve problems, improve efficiency, and make better decisions. I enjoy working with tools like Python, R, SQL, and Excel, but what motivates me most is finding ways to make the results useful to others.

Outside of academics and analytics, I'm an avid runner who enjoys training for marathons, playing ultimate frisbee, and constantly challenging myself to improve.

Ultimately, I'm someone who enjoys learning, solving problems, and taking on new challenges. Whether I'm analyzing a baseball game, developing a new project, or exploring an unfamiliar dataset, my goal is the same: turn data into meaningful insights that make a difference.
