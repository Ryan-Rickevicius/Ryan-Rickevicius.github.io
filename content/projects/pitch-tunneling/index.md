## The question

Pitch sequencing involves more than looking at one pitch at a time. For this research, I examined how changes in plate position between pitches relate to the probability of a swing and miss.

## Data and approach

I developed Plate Distance Differential, or PDD, to quantify plate-position change between pitches. The research used 2025 MLB Statcast data and a generalized linear mixed model to examine the relationship with whiff probability.

Dr. Deokkyung Ock advised the project at Quinnipiac University.

## My contribution

I developed the PDD metric and applied the statistical model. I also presented the research at the Tri-State Sport Management 2026 Annual Summit Poster Presentation in Hackensack, New Jersey.
